# 42195 Support

Thanks for using 42195! If you need help or have questions, here's how to get support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I connect my Strava account?
Go to the **Profile** tab and tap **Connect with Strava**. You'll be redirected to Strava to authorize the app.

### How do I sync my activities?
Activities sync automatically via Strava webhooks when you record a new run. You can also manually sync from the **Profile** tab by tapping **Sync with Strava**.

### How do I disconnect my Strava account?
You can revoke access at any time from your Strava settings:
1. Go to [strava.com/settings/apps](https://www.strava.com/settings/apps)
2. Find **42195** and click **Revoke Access**

### What data does 42195 access?
We only access your running activities: distance, duration, pace, heart rate, elevation, and route data. We do not access your social data, followers, or private messages. Your data is never shared or sold.

### How do I delete my data?
You can delete individual activities from the activity detail screen. To delete all your data and your account, please contact us by [opening an issue](https://github.com/VertexProtocol/42195/issues/new?template=account-request.yml).

## Get Help

If your question isn't answered above, please [open a support issue](https://github.com/VertexProtocol/42195/issues/new?template=support-request.yml) and we'll get back to you.

## Report a Bug

Found something broken? [Report a bug](https://github.com/VertexProtocol/42195/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) with steps to reproduce and we'll look into it.
